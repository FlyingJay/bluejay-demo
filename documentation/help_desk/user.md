# Help Desk (`help-desk`) — User Guide

Help Desk is where people ask a person for help. Use it for anything that isn't a request to build or change a feature: a problem you can't work out, a question the guides didn't answer, trouble with an order, or an account issue. Requests to change an app still go through Improve Apps; if a support request turns out to be a bug or an idea, the person answering can file it there for you.

Every group has its own **desk**, answered by the group's admins. Members, guests, clients and (when the group allows it) anyone on the web can send the group a request. The group's admins answer from a shared **Inbox**, and when they need help themselves they can ask the level above them: the group that manages theirs, or Bluejay support.

## Who can use it

| You are | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **A member** of a group that has Help Desk | Send requests to your group's admins, follow them, reply, and mark them solved. |
| **A signed-in guest or client portal client** | The same, once the group has turned on **Guests and clients** in its desk settings. |
| **Anyone, without an account** | Send a request from the group's public help form (`bluejay.gg/help/<group>`), once the group has turned it on. Replies arrive by email. |
| **An admin** of the group | Everything above, plus the **Inbox** of requests sent to your group, internal notes, saved replies, assigning, escalating, and the tab for your group's own requests to the level above. |
| **Bluejay support staff** (the Support role, or a platform admin) | The **Bluejay desk**: requests from staff groups and every request that reaches Bluejay from a group. |

If Help Desk isn't open to you in the current group, the app shows "You do not have access to Help Desk here."

## Words used in the app

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Request** (or ticket) | One conversation about one problem or question. |
| **Desk** | Where requests wait for an answer. Each group has one; Bluejay support has one more, the **Bluejay desk**. |
| **Requester** | Whoever sent the request: a person, or a group asking the level above it. |
| **Internal note** | A message only the people answering the desk see. Requesters never see notes. |
| **Escalate** | Ask the level above for help with a customer's request. It opens a linked request from your group; your customer keeps talking only to you. |
| **Topic** | The category a requester picks (Question, Problem, Account, Order, Other by default). |
| **Saved reply** | A prepared answer an admin can insert into a reply. |
| **Overdue** | A request still waiting for its first answer after the desk's first-reply target. |

## The tabs

What you see depends on what you can do in the group. If you have only one tab, the app shows the list without tabs.

### My requests

Requests you sent in this group. The **New request** button opens the form. Each row shows where the request went, its status, and how long ago something last happened. The tab's count is the number of your requests with a reply waiting for you.

### Inbox (admins)

Requests sent to your group's desk by members, guests, clients and the public form. Rows show who asked, who it's assigned to, and pills for **Overdue**, **High** or **Urgent** priority, and **Escalation** (a request that came from another group's escalation). The tab's count is the number of requests that need a reply.

If your group sends its requests straight to Bluejay support (staff groups do), the Inbox stays empty with the message "Requests in this group go straight to Bluejay support."

### Contact {Bluejay or your managing group} (admins)

Requests your group has sent to the level above it, including escalations. The **Contact …** button opens a form for something your group itself needs help with. Only your group's admins see these requests.

### Bluejay desk (support staff)

Requests from staff groups and every request that reaches Bluejay from another group. Rows show the group, and the person when it came from one. Requests to delete an account or a group's data also arrive here as tickets, with **Data & privacy** shown as the app in the request's details.

### Showing finished requests

Every tab shows open requests by default. Use the menu (⋯) and choose **Show resolved and closed** to see past ones, and **Show open requests** to go back. Pull down on a list to refresh it.

## Sending a request

1. Open Help Desk and tap **New request**, or use **Contact {group}** from any app's help tab (see below).
2. Write a short **Subject** (up to 120 characters) and describe the problem (up to 5,000 characters): what happened, what you expected, and what you already tried.
3. While you type, Help Desk may show **These guides might answer it** with links to matching app guides. Tap one to read it without leaving the form.
4. Pick a **Topic** if the group offers them, and a **Priority** (Normal or High; requests from a group to the level above can also be Urgent).
5. Tap **Send**. You'll see "Sent to {desk}. You'll be notified when they reply."

If the group has a welcome note, it appears at the top of the form.

### From another app's help tab

Every app has a help tab on the right edge of the screen. When you can send Help Desk requests in the current group, it offers **Contact {group}** ("Ask a person for help with this app") beside **How {app} works**. The request it opens is labelled **About {app}**, so whoever answers knows where you were. Guests and clients see this option too. If the group hasn't turned on requests from guests, tapping it explains "This group isn't taking requests here yet."

## Following a request

Open a request to see the whole conversation, oldest first. Your messages sit on the right. Grey lines in between are updates such as "Jamie marked it resolved."

What the status means to you:

| Status shown | What's happening |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for reply** | The desk has it and hasn't answered your latest message yet. |
| **Replied** | The desk answered and is waiting for you. |
| **Escalated** | The desk asked for more help and will follow up. |
| **Resolved** | Marked solved. It closes by itself 7 days later. |
| **Closed** | Finished. Replying reopens it. |

- **Reply** at the bottom. Any reply you send puts the request back with the desk.
- Tap **Solved** (top right) when you have what you need.
- A closed request still has a reply box ("Reply to reopen this request").

You get a notification (in the app, and by push, text or email per your notification settings) when the desk replies, resolves it, or escalates it. Tapping the notification opens the request.

### Finding a request from search

The launcher's search finds requests by subject or by the name of the person who sent them, including resolved and closed ones. It shows only requests you can already see in Help Desk for the current group: your own, plus the inbox and your group's requests to the level above if you're an admin. Bluejay desk requests don't appear there. Pick a result to open the request.

If your group uses Chat, a link to "where I am" sent while a request is open takes a teammate straight to that request, as long as they can see it in Help Desk.

## Answering requests (admins and support staff)

Open a request from the Inbox or the Bluejay desk. The header shows who sent it and from which group. A details card shows what's known about it: the requester's email (public-form requests), the app it was about, an order number, anything it was filed as, and a link to its escalation.

What the status means to the desk:

| Status shown | Whose move |
|---|---|
| **Needs reply** | Yours. |
| **Waiting on requester** | You answered; the requester hasn't replied yet. |
| **Escalated** | The level above is helping. |
| **Resolved** / **Closed** | Done. |

### Replying

- Type in the reply box and tap **Send**. The request moves to **Waiting on requester**.
- **Send and resolve** answers and marks it resolved in one step.
- Turn on **Internal note** to write something only your team sees. The button becomes **Add note** and the note shows in yellow with a lock. Notes don't change the status or notify the requester.
- **Saved replies** inserts one of your desk's prepared answers into the box (added to anything already typed). Edit it before sending if you like.

The first answer you send (not a note) counts as the desk's first response, which clears **Overdue**.

### The request menu (⋯)

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Assign** | Pick who on the desk handles it, or **Nobody**. The person you pick is notified. While it's assigned, the requester's replies notify only that person; otherwise everyone on the desk hears. |
| **Escalate to {level above}** | See below. Only on a group's desk, while the request is open, and once per request. |
| **File as a bug report** / **File as an idea** | Opens Improve Apps' own form with the request's app filled in. Once filed, the request lists it under **Filed as**. |
| **Mark resolved** | Resolves without a reply. |
| **Reopen** | Puts a resolved or closed request back to **Needs reply**. |
| **Close** | Finishes it now instead of waiting for the automatic close. |

### Escalating a customer's request

When your group can't solve something, escalate it to the level above (your managing group's desk, or Bluejay support):

1. Choose **Escalate to {level above}** in the request menu.
2. Write a summary of the problem and what you've tried.
3. Choose whether to **Include the conversation with {customer}**. Off (the default) shares only your summary. On adds the messages your customer could see. Internal notes are never shared.
4. Tap **Escalate**.

What happens next:

- A new request opens at the level above, sent by your group. It appears in your **Contact …** tab.
- Your customer sees "We've asked {level above} for help with this. We'll follow up here," and keeps talking only to you. They never see the level above directly.
- When the level above replies or resolves, a note with their answer appears on your customer's request (visible to your team only), so you can pass on what's useful.
- The original request stays **Escalated** while you keep talking to your customer. Resolve it yourself once the matter is settled.

### Contacting the level above for your own group

Admins can also ask the level above for help with something the group itself needs, without a customer request behind it: open the **Contact …** tab and tap **Contact {level above}**. Only your group's admins see these requests.

## Desk settings (admins)

Open the menu (⋯) and choose **Desk settings**. Changes save as you make them.

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Where requests go** | Platform admins only. Your group's admins, or straight to Bluejay support (used for staff groups). |
| **Who can ask** | **Guests and clients**: signed-in guests and client portal clients can send requests from Help Desk and every app's help tab. **Anyone, with the public help form**: people without an account can use `bluejay.gg/help/<group>`; replies go by email. When it's on, the form's link appears with a copy button. Members with Help Desk can always ask. |
| **Topics** | What requesters pick from. Up to 12, each up to 40 characters. At least one must stay. |
| **Saved replies** | Up to 30 prepared answers (title up to 60 characters, reply up to 2,000). Tap one to edit it. |
| **First reply within** | 4 hours, 8 hours, 1 day, 2 days or 3 days. Requests unanswered past this show **Overdue**. The default is 1 day. |
| **Welcome note** | Up to 500 characters shown at the top of the request form and the public form. Tap **Save note**. |

Support staff also have **Bluejay desk settings** in the menu, with saved replies and the first-reply target for the Bluejay desk.

## The public help form

When a group turns on the public form, anyone can reach it at `bluejay.gg/help/<group>` without signing in. The form, the emails and the request page show the group's legal name when the group has one set, otherwise its usual name.

1. Enter your name, email, a subject and your question. An order number and topic are optional. **Send** stays off until all four are filled in and the email is a complete address; if it isn't, the form shows "Enter a valid email address." under the email field once you move on from it.
2. Tap **Send**. The page confirms "We got your request" and you receive an email with a private link to it.
3. Each reply from the group arrives by email with a fresh link. Open it to read the conversation and reply, or tap **This solved it**.

Links work for 180 days. If one stops working, use the link in the most recent email about your request.

If the group's online store has the public form turned on, the store's order confirmation shows **Need help with this order?**, which opens the form with the order number filled in and the **Order** topic picked.

The public form limits how many requests can be sent from one connection and one email address in a day.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why, and what to do |
|---|---|
| "You do not have access to Help Desk here." | Help Desk isn't installed in this group, or you're a guest and the group hasn't turned on **Guests and clients**. Ask the group's admins. |
| "This group isn't taking requests here yet." | You tried **Contact {group}** from an app, but the group doesn't accept requests from you yet. |
| "You have opened a lot of tickets recently. Please try again later." | There's a limit of 20 requests an hour and 60 a day per person. Add to an existing request instead. |
| "This help page is not available" on the public form | The group hasn't turned on the public form, or the link is wrong. |
| "This link has expired or is not valid" | Use the link in the most recent email about your request. |
| "Too many requests from this email address today." | The public form takes 5 requests per email address a day. Try again tomorrow, or reply to one you already sent. |
| No **Escalate** option | The request is on the Bluejay desk (there's nothing above it), it's already escalated, or it's resolved or closed (reopen it first). |
| "Only platform admins can change where tickets go." | Routing can only be changed by a platform admin. |
| A member says they can't see a request they sent | They need Help Desk access in that group to see their requests. |

## Tips

- Check the guide suggestions before sending; the answer may already be written down.
- Use internal notes to hand a request over to a colleague without the customer seeing.
- Build saved replies for questions you answer every week.
- Escalate with the summary only unless the conversation itself is needed; it keeps your customer's details inside your group.
- Mark requests resolved when you're done. Anything resolved closes on its own after 7 days, and a reply from the requester reopens it.
